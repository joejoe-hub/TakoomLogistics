Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about our air and ocean freight services.

Should I choose Air Cargo or Ocean Cargo for my shipment?

It depends entirely on your timeline, budget, and cargo volume. Air Cargo is best for urgent, smaller, or high-value shipments, delivering in days. Ocean Cargo is much more economical for bulky, heavy, or massive shipments where transit speed is flexible (typically taking 45 to 60 days).

What are the typical delivery timelines?

Air freight typically arrives within 7–14 business days, while sea freight usually takes 45–60 days, depending on the destination and customs processing.

Are there weight or size minimums for shipping?

For Air Cargo, we accept smaller flexible weights calculated on simple per-kilogram scales. For Ocean Cargo, due to container consolidation parameters and port infrastructure fees, we maintain a clear 100kg baseline setup to ensure your distribution margins stay cost-effective.

Do you handle customs clearance documentation for both routes?

Yes, we handle the entire customs clearing process. Our experienced legal and clearing teams manage all required commercial invoices, air waybills, sea waybills, duties, and safety compliances at origin and destination terminals to ensure a completely stress-free handover.

Are there any restricted items that Takoom cannot ship?

Yes. Safety standards explicitly prohibit illegal contraband, explosives, and unstable corrosive liquids. Furthermore, international air safety protocols place much tighter restrictions on loose lithium batteries, aerosols, and pressurized containers compared to sea carrier lanes. Please reach out to our team before booking if you have any questions about specific materials.
